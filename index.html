The built environment is personal to me

I believe the spaces we build shape how people live, move, and connect — and that belief drives everything I do. As a construction management graduate student, I'm drawn to projects that leave something meaningful behind: infrastructure that serves communities, buildings that age with dignity, and spaces that people are proud to inhabit.

I'm equally fascinated by the systems behind the build — how smarter tools and fresh thinking can turn a good process into a great one. I'm always curious, always asking: how do we do this better?

Academic Projects

Developed a cost- and resource-loaded project schedule using Primavera P6 for a warehouse in Texas. Applied the Critical Path Method to sequence activities, assign resources, and identify schedule-critical tasks — producing a construction baseline ready for progress tracking.

Developed a comprehensive bid estimate and cost report in collaboration with peers for a 75,000+ SF correctional facility in upstate New York. The estimate encompassed direct costs, indirect costs, and contingencies — synthesizing quantity take-offs with market pricing to produce a competitive and defensible bid package.

Identified project risks, maintained a risk register, and performed qualitative and quantitative risk analyses using @RISK Monte Carlo simulations and Oracle Primavera. Gained insights into risk monitoring and developed mitigation strategies to minimize potential project impacts.
